## Facts
- The customer reports a broken safety seal, a bottle that was not full and a different chemical smell from an earlier bottle of the same serum.
- A second person used the product once and has a red rash. The customer kept the bottle and box.
- The customer asks what will be done and whether other bottles are affected.

## Why this tier and owner
Suspected tampering plus a skin reaction is a possible physical harm case. Product Quality owns it and needs it immediately.

## Constraints for the reply
- Do not guess at the cause. Do not say whether other bottles are affected.
- Do not promise compensation or admit fault.
- Advise stopping use. Ask for the batch code and photos of the seal, bottle and rash.
- Ask the customer to keep the bottle and box.

## Open questions
- Batch code and photos.
- Whether medical advice was sought.
