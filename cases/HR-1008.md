## Facts
- The customer reports the base of an Aura 1.7 kettle became too hot to touch after about 10 minutes. They touched it and have a blister on two fingers. No medical treatment sought yet.
- Bought 5 weeks ago. The customer still has the product and packaging.
- They ask for a refund and whether this is a known problem. They say another household has the same kettle with the same heat.
- HR-1005 is a press enquiry about the same product.

## Why this tier and owner
An injury report on a product. Product Quality owns it, and the match with HR-1005 makes a pattern possible.

## Constraints for the reply
- Do not admit fault or speculate on the cause. Do not say whether it is a known problem.
- Advise stopping use of the kettle. Ask for photos of the injury, the product and its serial number.
- Do not promise compensation.

## Open questions
- Serial number and photos.
- Whether medical treatment was sought.
