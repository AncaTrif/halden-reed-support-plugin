# General policy (fictional, simplified, not legal advice)

This is invented policy for the Halden & Reed portfolio project. Changes by pull request only.

## Authority

- Tier 2 may approve a goodwill refund or exception up to the market limit in the market file. Above the limit, Finance decides.
- Refund confirmations that depend on a payment record need a Finance check of that record first.
- A draft never decides an outcome. It states what the customer can expect next and who will decide.

## Safety, injury, tampering, hygiene

- Reply within the same day. Product Quality owns the case.
- Ask the customer to stop using the product. Ask for photos and the batch code or serial number. Ask them to keep the product and packaging.
- Do not guess at the cause. Do not admit fault. Do not say whether other units are affected or whether this is a known problem. Do not promise compensation.
- If someone is hurt, say that medical advice should come first and show concern in plain words.

## Legal threats

- Legal and GDPR owns the reply. A draft is a proposal for Legal, not something to send.
- Do not argue the terms, do not admit wording is misleading, do not promise a replacement, refund or any outcome.
- Acknowledge the message and say a colleague will respond. Stay calm and short.

## Press and media

- Support never replies to a journalist. Comms owns the reply.
- No comment on safety, reviews, recalls or sales figures. For press enquiries the output is an internal note for Comms only, with no customer-facing draft.

## Data subject requests (access, erasure, correction)

- Legal and GDPR owns the case. The statutory clock is one month from receipt.
- The reply acknowledges receipt only. It must not confirm deletion, refusal or any retention decision.

## Chargebacks

- Finance owns the response to the card scheme. Do not contact the customer before Finance decides.
- The output is an internal note for Finance with the order facts. No customer-facing draft.

## Payment errors

- Check the payment record before saying anything about a double charge or refund. Use the orders tool for the facts.
- Do not confirm a refund before Finance has checked the payment record.

## Delays and tracking

- Check the carrier status before giving a date. Do not promise a date unless the carrier confirms it.
- For repeat contacts, write a personal reply that explains what is known. No template wording. Offer the refund path when delivery cannot be guaranteed.

## Tone

- Plain, warm and short. Say what happens next and who does it.
- Write in the customer's language. Keep the customer's original language in any quoted text.
- No customer names, emails or addresses in internal notes. Greet the customer by the name in the ticket only inside the customer-facing draft.

## Never

- Never send anything. Drafts and internal notes only. A human presses send.
- Never invent facts about an order, a payment or a carrier. If a fact is missing, say it must be checked.
