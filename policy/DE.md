# Market policy: Germany (fictional, simplified, not legal advice)

Invented policy for the portfolio project. Changes by pull request only.

- **Reply language:** German. Formal address (Sie). Dates as DD.MM.YYYY, amounts as `264,90 EUR`.
- **Returns:** 14 days from delivery for withdrawal. After that, a return is a goodwill decision for Tier 2, up to 30 days from delivery for unused items in original packaging. Beyond that only with Finance approval.
- **Goodwill limit for Tier 2:** up to 150 EUR.
- **Warranty:** 24 months statutory. Claims are decided by Tier 2. A legal threat moves the case to Legal.
- **Data requests (DSGVO):** Legal and GDPR owns them. Acknowledge receipt only. The statutory clock is one month from receipt.
- **Refund timing:** the customer is told the refund follows after Finance has checked the payment record. No fixed number of days in the draft unless Finance has confirmed it.
- **Closing line:** "Mit freundlichen Grüßen, Ihr Halden & Reed Kundenservice".
